## 1. The March 2025 deals: what Jio and Airtel signed with SpaceX, and where they stand

### Takeaway
Both agreements were conditional and mostly exploratory. They were signed a day apart (Airtel on 11 Mar 2025, Jio on 12 Mar 2025), and each depended on SpaceX first getting its own Indian authorisation. Jio committed to retail and online sales of Starlink kits and to a "mechanism" for installation and activation support. Airtel's release covered retail sales, business customers and rural community connections, and said the two would explore network and ground-infrastructure use. Neither release disclosed a revenue share. No source reports either deal being cancelled, but neither can operate while Starlink cannot sell. Neither Jio nor Airtel commented on the deals during the feud.

They "will also explore how Starlink could help expand and enhance the Airtel network, as well as SpaceX's ability to utilize and benefit from Airtel's ground network infrastructure and other capabilities in India". ## 2. Frenemies: how Jio and Airtel also compete with Starlink

### Takeaway
Each incumbent has its own satellite business that competes with Starlink. Jio has three: a 51:49 joint venture with SES (GEO plus MEO satellites), Jio Satellite Communications (GMPCS licence, 2022), and a proposed sovereign constellation of about 1,600 LEO satellites shown at IMC 2026, with a test flight targeted for 2027. Airtel's group owns about 17.8% of Eutelsat, the owner of OneWeb, which was licensed in India in 2021. OneWeb focuses on business and government customers. Amazon's Kuiper (now Amazon Leo) must reapply in India and is looking for local partners such as Tata's Nelco.

## 3. Timing: what was pending when Musk posted, and the SpaceX, Trump and Modi backdrop

### Takeaway
The posts landed in a crowded week. At IMC 2026, Starlink, Jio and Airtel all pitched satellite plans, and Jio showed its 1,600-satellite plan. DoT was drafting the Cabinet note on satcom spectrum pricing; its top body, the DCC, had approved a 5%-of-revenue charge on 3 Sep. The MHA security review had no deadline, and Bloomberg had reported in June that Starlink's approval was effectively frozen over terminals used in Iran. Jio filed its prospectus on 15 Oct, and the IPO opens 21–23 Oct. Rubio's 17–22 Oct trip skips Delhi for Mumbai and Reliance's Jamnagar, while US–India trade talks are stalled. SpaceX's share price was falling. SpaceX's prospectus says nothing specific about Starlink in India. Musk has reconciled with Trump, and his relationship with Modi had been warm.

- [Fact] Spectrum price. The DCC, DoT's top decision-making body, approved a spectrum usage charge of 5% of adjusted gross revenue (AGR) at its 3 Sep meeting, cut to 4% in notified remote, hilly, island and border areas. ## 4. History: the 2024 spectrum fight, the 2025–26 pricing fight, and Reliance's lobbying

### Takeaway
In October 2024, Jio (and, at IMC, Mittal) argued for auctions, and Starlink and Kuiper for administrative allocation. ## 7. Why Ambani rather than Mittal

### Takeaway
Musk's first post ("certain oligarchs", plural) was read as aimed at both men. Every later post named only Ambani. The evidence-backed reasons:
1. **Size:** Jio is the largest operator, with 530 million+ customers.
2. **Home broadband:** Jio is the home-broadband incumbent Starlink's consumer product would hit, while OneWeb sells to businesses and governments.
3. **Old grudge:** Musk's 2024 public fight was with Reliance's auction lobbying.
4. **Timing:** Jio unveiled a rival constellation the day Musk first posted.
5. **IPO platform:** Jio's IPO and its risk section on Starlink drew attention.
6. **Political profile:** Musk himself linked Reliance to political donations.

On Mittal's side, Airtel was SpaceX's first Indian partner, and SpaceX praised its role. I found no reporting of a personal Musk–Mittal friendship, and no public reply from Musk to Mittal's "ahead in the queue" dig.

## 1. SEBI asked brokers, analysts and IPO platforms to stop publishing grey market premiums, calling GMP 'unofficial, unverified and unregulated', on the day Mahanadi Coalfields' IPO closed with a ₹0.45 GMP

Hot lane (new; SEBI advisory Mon 23 Nov evening; tied to the tracked Mahanadi Coalfields IPO, which lists Fri 27 Nov) · RISING · **9/10** (momentum 2/3, stake 1/1, shareability 2/2, gap 2/2, known name 2/2) · tracker id `sebi-gmp-advisory`

Window: Post by Thu 26 Nov. Mahanadi lists Fri 27 Nov (allotment Wed 25 Nov), and Crystal Crop Protection lists Thu 26 Nov: the first listings after the advisory. Watch for NSE/BSE circulars forwarding it.

### What's happening
- On Monday 23 Nov SEBI issued a press release (PR No. 75/2026) asking all registered intermediaries and market participants to stop publishing, displaying or broadcasting IPO grey market premium figures, on websites, apps, email, SMS and social media (Tribune/ANI; Marketcalls).
- Its reason: GMP figures are 'unofficial, unverified and unregulated', shared widely as if they predict listing prices or returns. GMP deals happen outside the exchanges, with no settlement guarantee and no investor protection (Tribune/ANI).
- Investors in GMP-linked trades get no investor protection and cannot use the exchanges' grievance system or SEBI's ODR portal (Tribune/ANI; Marketcalls).
- It is an advisory: no effective date, no penalty clause and no regulation cited, and it says nothing to newspapers or unregistered websites. By Monday night no NSE or BSE circular had forwarded it (Marketcalls).
- Same day, the year's biggest PSU IPO closed: Mahanadi Coalfields (Coal India selling 10%, ₹8,981 crore, price band ₹129-136) ended 1.52x subscribed, carried by institutions at 4.95x while retail filled only 46% of its quota (BusinessToday, BSE data).
- Its GMP slid from ₹3.65 on 17 Nov to ₹0.70 on 22 Nov and ₹0.45 on 23 Nov (IIFL), about 49 rupees a lot. Sources disagree: an earlier tracker reading had it near ₹25 before the issue opened, and Goodreturns printed a 'retail GMP of ₹100'. The disagreement is SEBI's point. Listing Fri 27 Nov.

### The curiosity gap

## 3. Thirteen years after the NSEL default, 5,682 investors were paid ₹1,950 crore on Monday: 42.34% of their admitted claims

### What's happening
- On Monday 23 Nov the National Spot Exchange (NSEL) released a ₹1,950 crore one-time settlement to 5,682 claimants, under a scheme approved by the NCLT in Mumbai; 92.81% of claimants by number and 91.35% by value had voted for it (Free Press Journal; webindia123/PTI).

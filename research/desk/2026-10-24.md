## 1. SEBI's final order: Avadhut Sathe must refund ₹546 crore of course fees, plus ₹68 crore interest, and is out of the market for two years

Hot lane (new; SEBI order, famous name) · RISING · **9/10** (momentum 2/3, stake 1/1, shareability 2/2, gap 2/2, known name 2/2) · tracker id `avadhut-sathe-refund`

Window: Sat 24 Oct to about Fri 30 Oct. Next turns: whether Sathe appeals to the Securities Appellate Tribunal, and the newspaper notice on how participants claim refunds (due within 30 days).

### What's happening
- SEBI passed a final order on Fri 23 Oct (SEBI's orders page lists 'Order in the matter of Avadhut Sathe Trading Academy Private Limited', 23 Oct). It follows the interim order of 4 Dec 2025.
- The academy and Avadhut Sathe must refund ₹546.17 crore collected from participants for what SEBI calls unregistered investment advice and research, plus ₹67.87 crore interest (ETV Bharat, 23 Oct, 10:34 pm).
- Penalties ₹11.5 crore in all: the academy ₹6 crore, Avadhut Sathe ₹4 crore, his wife Gouri Sathe ₹1.5 crore (ETV Bharat).
- Bans: the academy and Sathe are barred from the securities market for two years or until they finish the refund, whichever is later; Gouri Sathe for six months, for failing to exercise oversight (ETV Bharat).
- The academy collected about ₹601.38 crore from course participants since it began; SEBI counted ₹546.17 crore as fees for the programmes in question (ETV Bharat).
- SEBI's findings: advice and stock recommendations given through paid programmes, sessions and WhatsApp groups without registration; misleading promotional claims about returns; advice given without checking whether it suited the people receiving it (ETV Bharat).
- Mechanics: refunds within five months of the order; a newspaper notice on how to claim within 30 days; bank accounts stay frozen for debits until the money is deposited (ETV Bharat).
- Context (Dec 2025, older than a week, so context only): the interim order impounded ₹546.16 crore and barred Sathe 'until further notice'; SEBI had warned him in March 2024 and searched the Karjat academy in August 2025 (Outlook Money; The Week). The academy said it is purely a training institute and does not give tips, and said it would challenge the order (Business Standard via search summary).

### The curiosity gap
- He was famous for dancing on stage at his trading seminars. Where did ₹601 crore of fees come from, and who gets ₹546 crore back?
- SEBI didn't fine him for losing people's money in trades. It is making him return the course fees. What exactly was he selling?
- People paid him to learn to beat the market. Now SEBI adds ₹68 crore of interest on their fees: did the fees earn more than the trades?

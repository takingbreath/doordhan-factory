## 1. The Supreme Court closes the unclaimed-money case: Rs 86,917 crore of forgotten deposits sits with the RBI, and heirs still have to search portal by portal

Deep lane (Moneylife and Sucheta Dalal's PIL; ruled Fri 27 Nov) · RISING (news); COLD on YouTube · **8/10** (momentum 1/3, stake 1/1, shareability 2/2, gap 2/2, known name 2/2) · tracker id `unclaimed-assets-sc`

Window: Evergreen, but the ruling is news this week: post by Wed 2 Dec while it is fresh. 14-day deep-lane window.

### What's happening
- On Fri 27 Nov a Supreme Court bench of Justices Vikram Nath and R Mahadevan disposed of Sucheta Dalal vs Union of India (filed 2022). The PIL asked for one central, searchable database so heirs and nominees could find a dead relative's unclaimed bank deposits, insurance, shares and mutual funds (Bar and Bench).
- The court found the 'principal concerns' substantially addressed by what the authorities have done. It recorded their assurances and gave no further directions. Specific grievances go to the competent authority (Bar and Bench).
- What the Centre told the court on 17 Nov: Rs 6,453 crore of unclaimed financial assets had been returned to their owners as of 18 Apr 2026; over 3 lakh claims were processed to March 2026, about 10% still pending; and up to four nominees are now allowed after the 2025 banking-law amendment (LiveLaw, 17 Nov).
- So heirs keep searching portal by portal: the RBI's UDGAM for bank deposits (30 banks enrolled, about 90% of unclaimed deposits by value), the IEPF portal for shares and dividends, and SEBI's MITRA for mutual funds (LiveLaw, 17 Nov).
- Context: the RBI's Depositor Education and Awareness (DEA) Fund held Rs 86,917.08 crore of unclaimed bank deposits at 30 June 2026. SBI's share was Rs 20,040.44 crore, the largest. The RBI had reimbursed Rs 17,414.68 crore to banks for claims paid (Rajya Sabha answer of 11 Aug, Outlook Money). This is context, older than a week, but it is the latest official figure.

### The curiosity gap
- Rs 86,917 crore is sitting unclaimed with the RBI, and the Supreme Court has just said the system for finding it is good enough. Is some of it your family's?
- One bank, SBI, accounts for Rs 20,040 crore of it. Why does money go unclaimed in India's biggest bank?
- Diwali cleaning turned up Reliance share certificates worth lakhs this month (9M views). What else is lying in the names of our parents and grandparents, and where do you even look?

## 1. Jio IPO is official: opens 21 Oct, a ₹2,500 crore quota for anyone who held Reliance shares on 14 Oct

Hot lane (tracked story, new turn: dates official) · HOT · **8/10** (momentum 3/3, stake 1/1, shareability 1/2, gap 1/2, known name 2/2) · tracker id `jio-ipo`

Window: Thu 15 Oct to Fri 23 Oct (subscription closes); the story runs again at listing on 28 Oct.

### What's happening
- New since we last sent it: Reliance's filing confirms the dates (PTI, 14 Oct). Anchor bids Mon 19 Oct; subscription Wed 21 to Fri 23 Oct; listing expected 28 Oct.
- It is a fresh issue of up to 27 crore shares (₹10 face value), with no offer for sale: the money goes to the company, not to Reliance, Meta or Google. Reports put the raise at about ₹30,000 crore (₹30,213 crore at the top of a reported ₹1,065–1,119 band). The band is not in the filing; it comes with the RHP.
- Use of money: up to ₹27,500 crore to repay or prepay borrowings of Reliance Jio Infocomm (Kotak Neo, 13 Oct), the rest for general corporate purposes.
- Two reserved quotas: up to ₹2,500 crore for eligible Reliance shareholders (individuals and HUFs holding Reliance shares as of 14 Oct) and up to ₹500 crore for Jio Platforms employees.
- Who owns it now: Reliance 66.43%; Meta and Google about 17.7% together. FY26: revenue ₹1,46,885 crore (+14.6%), net profit ₹30,049 crore (+15.1%), EBITDA margin 51.91%; 524 million+ subscribers; ARPU ₹214 a month in the March 2026 quarter (draft prospectus, via PTI).

### The curiosity gap
- If you held even a few Reliance shares on Tuesday, you're in a separate ₹2,500 crore queue for India's biggest IPO. Most Reliance holders probably don't know they're in it.
- India's biggest IPO, and not a rupee goes to Ambani, Meta or Google: about ₹27,500 crore of the ~₹30,000 crore pays down Jio's loans. So what exactly are IPO buyers paying for?
- Jio earns ₹214 a month from the average user and kept a 52% EBITDA margin. Your monthly recharge is the business being sold.

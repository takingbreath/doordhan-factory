## 2. Binance lost $6.3 billion in about 20 minutes, the biggest crypto theft ever, and says its users won't pay a rupee. When WazirX lost about $230 million in 2024, Indian users did

Hot lane (new) · HOT · **9/10** (momentum 3/3, stake 1/1, shareability 2/2, gap 1/2, known name 2/2) · tracker id `binance-hack-wazirx`

Window: About 3 to 5 days. Withdrawals resumed Sun 1 Nov; Binance planned a proof of reserves for Mon 2 Nov; the FBI named North Korea on Saturday. The next turns are the reserves report and how much of the remaining 10% is covered.

### What's happening
- On Thu 29 Oct, starting between 4:05 and 4:20 pm ET, about $6.3 billion moved out of Binance's hot wallets across 10 blockchains, most of it in about 20 minutes: about $3.8 billion in Ether and $1.5 billion in Bitcoin (18,270 BTC). It is the largest crypto theft on record, about four times Bybit's $1.5 billion in Feb 2025 (Wikipedia, citing CNBC and others; CNBC, 31 Oct).
- How: Binance first pointed to a DevOps engineer's compromised laptop; co-CEO Richard Teng later said it was more complex than one engineer's permissions. The FBI on Saturday attributed it to North Korea's TraderTraitor group (Wikipedia; NPR, 31 Oct).
- About $3 billion in stablecoins was frozen on-chain by Tether, Circle and Ripple; the $1.5 billion in Bitcoin could not be (Wikipedia, citing CNBC, 30 Oct). Bitcoin fell about 5%, below $80,000 (Wikipedia).
- Binance: 'Binance will absorb the financial impact of these stolen funds, not our users.' By Saturday it said 90% of the stolen assets were covered (the top ten coins fully backed; the rest is less liquid tokens), using its SAFU user fund, part of its futures insurance fund and its corporate treasury. Withdrawals resumed on Sunday (CNBC, 31 Oct, updated 1 Nov). CNN puts coverage at about 95%.
- The Indian contrast (context, older than a week): in July 2024 hackers took about $230 million from WazirX; its 16 million users had their accounts frozen and trading stayed shut for about 15 months, until a Singapore court approved a restructuring that does not repay them in full. Binance denied any responsibility for WazirX (Cointelegraph; Entrepreneur India).

### The curiosity gap
- The world's biggest crypto exchange lost about ₹53,000 crore in 20 minutes and its users lost nothing. An Indian exchange lost a twenty-fifth of that and its users paid for it. What's the difference?
- One engineer's laptop. That's where a $6.3 billion theft started, by Binance's own first account.
- Your money on an exchange isn't in your wallet. Who pays when it's stolen depends on who you trusted.

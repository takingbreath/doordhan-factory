# sk05 eMudhra — Akhilesh's script v1 (22 Oct 2026), Pandey monologue, no caller

India ki is security company ne yeh kya kaand kar diya? Chutiya [beeped] log!

eMudhra do lower circuits mein chhattis percent gir gaya.

GST, income tax aur import-export registration tak mein eMudhra ka signing software use hota hai!

Toh ee company website asli hone ka digital certificate deti hai—samjho, digital mohar. Socho, agar uss mohar ka password ek-do-teen ho! Matlab hackers nakli certificate bana ke nakli website bhi asli dikha sakte the.

Pichhle saal isi system mein gadbad ki warning aayi thi. Company kehti hai, email miss ho gayi. Follow-up aaya, toh action liya—lekin problem ki jad nahi pakdi.

Company khud kehti hai, ee problem kareeb atharah mahine aur do audits ke bad bhi nahi pakdi gayi. Toh audit kis cheez ki ho rahi thi?

Company kehti hai, data breach nahi hua, India ki signing services unaffected hain. Matlab, sab changa si?

Ab eMudhra website-certificate business hi chhod rahi hai. Lekin ab customer kisi bhi kaam ke liye tumpe bharosa kare toh kaise kare? Sawaal poore process pe hai.

Do hazaar gyarah mein Netherlands ki company DigiNotar hack hui thi. Nakli certificates bhi istemaal hue the. Aur maamla public hone ke kareeb teen hafte baad company bankrupt ho gayi thi.

Company kehti hai, ee business revenue ka sirf ek percent hai. Par sawaal poori company ki credibility ka hai.

Tabhi toh main kehta hoon…

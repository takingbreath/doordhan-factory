## v2 changes (Akhilesh, 22 Oct: Jio reel is the benchmark for reality; The Office-style camera; not deadpan; the AI-realism PDF)
- Look: modern documentary grade like jio01 (1080x1920, 30 fps, light grain, warmth), no CRT/kendra finish.
- Keyframes rebuilt photoreal on seedream_v4_5 with the PDF's five pillars: named light (cool overhead tubelight mixing with warm
  late-afternoon daylight through a barred window, camera left), materials (chipped enamel board, dented grey steel almirah with hard
  reflections, cotton shirt folds, laminated desk with tea rings, files tied with faded red cloth), depth (a foreground file stack or
  door-frame edge out of focus, the wall softer behind), motion (mid-word, mid-exhale), expression (asymmetric, unposed), and a camera
  block per frame (eye level, about 1.5-2 m, 35 mm, f/2.8; a documentary operator, not a tripod).
- Performance: openly frustrated, expressive (knitted brows, small incredulous head shakes, an exhale, an open-palm gesture on the punch
  lines, no objects), eyes mostly on the lens; one Office-style "look to camera" on "sab changa si?".
- Camera (The Office): handheld operator sway on every shot, snap zooms with overshoot on the punch lines, whip pans between Pandey,
  Billu and the collage, shots through a foreground (door frame, file stack), late reframes, a focus hunt after each zoom. All in the
  edit (Wan stays locked-off; camera moves inside Wan cost lip articulation). Test: work/sk05/camtest/camtest_mockumentary.mp4.
- Talking shots framed chest-up (face 24-28% of frame height) so a 1.5x snap zoom has room; T2 and T4 (the biggest zooms) at 1080p.
- Cost: keyframes ~7, talking 45.5 (T2, T4 at 1080p), silents 13.5 = ~66; ask for up to 75 with one retake.

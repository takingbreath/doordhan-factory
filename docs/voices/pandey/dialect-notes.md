Source: his lines (speaker_0) in the two reference transcripts, without Dupahiya lines 16 and 25 (the father's): 82 sentences, 517 words.
"Dupahiya" is reference/laapataa-dupahiya-gajraj-rao-ravi-kishan-sparsh-shrivastava-prime-video-india.md and "Ladies" is reference/ravi-kishans-unexpected-heroic-moment-ft-pratibha-ranta-laapataa-ladies-netflix-india.md; numbers are their line numbers.
Spellings are Scribe's. "Where it sits" is the place in the sentence; a leading अरे, अबे, ए or हाँ doesn't count as the start.

4. **"hum" or "tum" with a past verb and no "ne": found, 4** (hum 2, tum 2; counted only where standard Hindi needs ने). Start 2, middle 2. Spelling हम/तुम with the verb in -ए (किए, लौटाए), once बोला. "हम आपको लौटाए" (Ladies 35), "हम paperwork किए" (Ladies 7), "तुम … बोला" (Dupahiya 22), "तुम परेशान किए" (Ladies 63).

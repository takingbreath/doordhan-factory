# Platform playbook: how Reels and Shorts distribute, and the rules we stay inside

Maintained by the strategist routine: it checks for changes every Sunday and updates this file, with a source for every line. Researched 2026-09-25; checked 2026-10-04 (no change to Reels ranking, Shorts policy, SEBI's finfluencer rules or India's AI labels; the April draft on continuous AI labels is still unconfirmed as final: [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/news/tighter-norms-meity-proposes-continuous-label-visibility-on-full-duration-of-ai-generated-content)). Items marked "unconfirmed" had no official source.

## Instagram: what gets reach
- **The three signals Instagram names:** watch time, likes per reach and **sends per reach**. Sends (DM shares) matter most for reaching non-followers; likes matter more with followers. (Mosseri, Jan 2025: [SMT](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/))
- **The first 3 seconds.** Instagram's own advice; Insights show skip rate (people who leave within 3 s) and a retention curve. ([Creators FAQ](https://creators.instagram.com/faq?locale=en_US), [SMT](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-retention-insights-reels/758464/)) Our first two reels: 54% and 67% skip.
- **Trial Reels** are shown to non-followers first and can be shared to followers automatically if they do well within 72 h. Use them to test hooks. ([IG](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels))
- **Originality:** no other apps' watermarks, no reposts. Accounts that mostly repost lost recommendations from 30 Apr 2026. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/))
- **Hashtags:** at most 5 per post (Dec 2025). ([@creators](https://www.threads.com/@creators/post/DSalXGPCWM4/new-hashtag-guidance-starting-today-instagram-will-allow-up-to-hashtags-in-a))
- **Frequency:** the fastest-growing creators average 10+ Reels a month. Reels up to 3 minutes are recommended. (Creators FAQ)
- **"Your Algorithm"** (2025–26) lets viewers edit their topics, so a clear, consistent topic helps. ([SEL](https://searchengineland.com/instagram-your-algorithm-465987))
- **Engagement bait is demoted:** posts that explicitly ask for comments, shares or tags ("send this to…", "comment SIP for…"). Make the reel sendable instead; ask real open questions. ([Meta](https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/content-distribution-guidelines/engagement-bait/))

## Instagram: what makes a reel ineligible for recommendations
It stays visible to followers but isn't shown to anyone else. ([Recommendation Guidelines](https://help.instagram.com/313829416281232))
- **Sexually suggestive content:** the guidelines name "pictures of people in see-through clothing" and partial nudity. Meta also demotes "a person posing in a sexually suggestive manner". ([Meta](https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/types-of-content-we-demote/)) Cleavage isn't named, but framing on a body part is the risk. So: no sheer or see-through fabric as the main garment, no chest-focused framing or slow zooms over the body, no suggestive poses. Glamour comes from styling, make-up, hair, set and light.
- **Money claims:** Meta removes "guaranteed or risk-free" returns, past performance used to suggest future returns, and "quick returns". ([Meta](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/fraud-scams/))
- **AI:** disclose photorealistic AI video and audio with the "AI info" label. **Since 31 Aug 2026, accounts that feature an AI-generated person without the "AI-generated profile" label lose recommendations to non-followers**; labelled profiles don't. ([IG](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/ai-generated-profile-label), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/instagram-puts-new-limits-on-undisclosed-ai-profiles/))
- **Where the profile label lives:** Profile → Edit profile → "AI-generated profile". It's rolling out and wasn't in our app on 4 Oct; Account Status flags an unlabelled AI profile under Recommendation eligibility if Instagram detects one. ([Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/tech/instagram-ai-generated-profile-label-reach-restrictions))
- **Monetisation "Established presence"** in Account Status means the account is too new or small for monetisation tools; it isn't a reach restriction. Requesting a review is allowed once every 90 days, so wait until we have the followers.
- **Account Status** (Settings → Account Status) shows whether the account and its posts can be recommended, and lets you appeal. Check it weekly. ([Help](https://help.instagram.com/653964212890722))

## YouTube Shorts
- **What drives distribution:** "viewed vs swiped away", viewing behaviour and satisfaction. Views count on every play since 31 Mar 2025. ([Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12942217))
- **Disclose altered or synthetic content** on every upload. Since 27 May 2026 YouTube also labels undisclosed photorealistic AI itself; the label alone doesn't change reach. ([Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491), [blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/improving-ai-labels-viewers-creators/))
- **Inauthentic content** (mass-produced, template-looking, including AI) is policed. Vary formats between episodes. ([YPP policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392))
- **YPP rewrite (announced 10 Aug 2026, effective 1 Feb 2027):** new-applicant thresholds double (8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views in 90 days), and inauthentic-content enforcement (synthetic narration, templated formats) has run in waves since Jan 2026. For us: vary formats and keep each reel visibly made, not templated. ([AIR](https://air.io/en/monetization/youtube-monetization-policy-changes-2026-a-complete-dated-timeline)) Checked 2026-09-27.
- **Monetisation: an AI persona giving finance information cannot be monetised**, even when disclosed (policy of Jul 2025, clarified Jul 2026). Treat YouTube as reach and audience, not ad revenue. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/20/youtube-clarifies-policies-around-ai-slop-and-upsetting-videos/))

## India
- **SEBI (finfluencers):**
  - No advice, recommendations or return claims. Education is fine. ([Jan 2025 circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/jan-2025/details-clarifications-on-provisions-related-to-association-of-persons-regulated-by-the-board-miis-and-their-agents-with-persons-engaged-in-prohibited-activities_91356.html))
  - **Price data:** from 1 Jul 2026, don't use price data from the last 30 days while naming or showing a stock in a way that implies direction or a recommendation. ([SEBI, May 2026](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/may-2026/norms-for-sharing-and-usage-of-price-data-for-educational-purposes_101293.html))
  - Regulated firms can't pay or promote creators who do prohibited activities; this matters for any future sponsorship.
  - Enforcement is real (Avadhut Sathe, ₹546 cr, Dec 2025).
- **IT Rules 2026 on synthetic content** (in force 20 Feb 2026): AI content must carry a "prominent, easily noticeable" label, and platforms collect the uploader's declaration and apply it. So always declare AI when posting. ([Khaitan](https://www.khaitanco.com/thought-leadership/MeitY-notifies-the-IT-Amendment-Rules-2026))
  - **Draft (Apr 2026, consultation closed 7 May; final status unconfirmed as of 27 Sep):** labels on AI video must be "continuous and clearly visible throughout the duration", with an up-front audio disclosure; the duty sits on platforms, and users may not strip labels. A small on-screen "AI" mark burnt into our own frame would already comply. ([MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/04/223-meity-ai-label-rules-mandates-continuous-disclosure/))
- **ASCI:** virtual influencers must disclose they're not human in promotional posts, and investment-advice influencers need SEBI registration. ([ASCI](https://www.ascionline.in/social/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/ASCI-Guidelines-Influencer-Advertising-In-Digital-Media-1.pdf))

## Checklist for every reel
1. Instagram: the AI-generated profile label is on, and the reel is tagged as AI. YouTube: altered/synthetic is ticked.
2. No buy/sell/hold, targets, return claims, "guaranteed" or "risk-free". No price data under 30 days old next to a named stock in a directional way.
3. Sarika: no see-through main garment, no chest-focused framing or body zooms, no suggestive poses.
4. The hook lands in 1–2 s, on screen and in the voice. Pay off by about 20 s, with a loop back to the start if possible.
5. Sendable by design, with no "share/comment/tag" asks in the reel or caption.
6. 100% original, no other apps' watermarks, at most 5 hashtags, a keyword-rich caption.
7. Test hooks with Trial Reels while the account is small. Aim for 10+ reels a month.
8. Check Account Status weekly.

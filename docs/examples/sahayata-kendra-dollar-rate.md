# Example: Sahayata Kendra, "Dollar ka rate kaun tay karta hai?"

PANDEY: Dollar ka rate koi tay nahi karta.

[TRRRING]

CALLER: Hello, Doordhan Sahayata Kendra? Pandey ji, ek cheez samajh nahi aati. Yeh dollar ka rate roz badal jaata hai. Decide kaun karta hai? Sarkar? RBI? Koi toh baitha hoga upar?

PANDEY: Tumko lagta hai upar koi kursi pe baitha rate likhta hai. Koi nahi baitha. Hum dekh aaye hain. Bank log din bhar dollar khareedte-bechte hain, aur jis daam pe sauda ho gaya, wahi rate hai. Jab dollar lene waale zyada aur dene waale kam, toh dollar mehenga aur tumra rupaya neeche. Ka karoge, market hai. Ab lene waala kaun? Sabse pehle tel waala, oil importer, kyunki tel dollar mein aata hai. Phir foreign investor. Ee jab ghabraata hai, rupaya bech ke apna paisa dollar mein nikaal le jaata hai. RBI bhi isi market mein ek khiladi hai, bas bahut bada wala. Rupaya tez girne lage toh oo apne reserves se dollar bechta hai. Kaahe? Kaahe ki ekdum gira toh petrol, dawai, bahar ka har saamaan, sab ek saath mehenga. Toh RBI rate tay nahi karta. Bas girna dheema karta hai.

BILLU: [Mez pe pada ek rupaye ka sikka, paw se, bahut dheere-dheere kinaare tak khiskaata hai. Sikka gir jaata hai. Billu jhuk ke neeche dekhta hai.]

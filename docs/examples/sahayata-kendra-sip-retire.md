# Example: Sahayata Kendra, "How much SIP is enough to retire?"

Akhilesh's pick from the first loop v0.3 run, 3 Oct 2026 (sip-retire-1, w1-2). What to take from it: it answers the caller's own case with the caller's own number, and every figure comes out of the one before (three crore, then what that buys today, then what that gives a month, then "das hazaar ka SIP, pandrah hazaar ka kharcha"). The other two picks taught the whole method and were not followed. Its known weakness: the first line, "Teen crore utna bada nahi hai", names a number the viewer hasn't heard yet; a first line has to stand on its own. Don't reuse its lines.

**PANDEY**: Teen crore utna bada nahi hai.
**CALLER**: Hello, Doordhan Sahayata Kendra? Pandey ji, main tees ka hoon, das hazaar ka SIP chalta hai. Dost bolta hai saath tak teen crore. Retirement ke liye kaafi hai na?
**PANDEY**: Dost ka hisaab theek hai, baarah percent return maan ke teen crore banega. Par oo tees saal baad ka teen crore hai. Tab tak daam badhte rahenge. Mehngai chhe percent maan ke, aaj ke hisaab se ee lagbhag aadha crore hai. Aur ee aadha crore ek baar mein nahi, saath se nabbe tak kharchna hai. Toh mahina milega, aaj ke hisaab se, kareeb pandrah hazaar. Matlab das hazaar ka SIP, pandrah hazaar ka kharcha. Pandrah hazaar mein ghar chal jaata hai, toh badhai ho, kaafi hai. Nahi chalta, toh hisaab seedha hai: mahine ka jitna kharcha, uska do-tihaai SIP. Tumra dost teen crore tak gina, uske aage nahi.
**BILLU**: [Bade se katore mein muh daalta hai. Doodh bas tali mein thoda sa hai. Katore ko der tak dekhta rehta hai.]

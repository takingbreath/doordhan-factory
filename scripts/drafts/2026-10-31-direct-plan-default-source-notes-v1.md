## Cost mechanism — primary sources

Opened [AMFI's Direct Plan explanation](https://www.amfiindia.com/investor/knowledge-center-info?zoneName=DirectPlan): direct and regular share a scheme portfolio and manager; direct excludes distributor/agent commissions and therefore has a lower expense ratio. AMFI also explains the role of distributor assistance and the capability needed for independent selection. This is not evidence that every distributor provides valuable advice or that direct has no costs.

Opened [HDFC Mutual Fund's Direct Plan FAQ](https://www.hdfcfund.com/services/faqs/introduction-direct-plan): explains separate plans, switch requests and possible exit-load/tax consequences. Parts explicitly concern the 2013 introduction of direct plans, so use them for mechanics only, not as a new 2026 implementation notice or a universal exit-load rule.

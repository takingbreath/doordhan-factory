## Current primary rule

[RBI, NBFC Governance Directions 2025, updated 24 June 2026](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12960).

Read paragraph 43: upper-layer NBFCs must list within three years of identification. Paragraph 3(3) applies paragraphs 41–43 to registered CICs, subject to layer. This supplies the regulatory link; it is not a direct new RBI order addressed to Tata Sons. No claim is made about an exact sanction, immediate enforcement date, or the unpublished reasoning behind refusal of surrender.

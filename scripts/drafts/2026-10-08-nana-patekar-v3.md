## English script — one narrator

Nana Patekar had ₹1.5 crore set aside for a car. Then he watched the news.

It was about farmers who had died by suicide—and the families they left behind.

He dropped the car plan and decided to help those families instead. ₹15,000 cheques were distributed.

He also co-founded Naam Foundation with Makarand Anaspure. The work included support for farmers' families and water conservation.

Nana Patekar died today. This is one choice worth remembering: money set aside for a car became a way to help someone else.

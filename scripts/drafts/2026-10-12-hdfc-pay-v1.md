# HDFC CEO package — headline versus payslip

Status: DRAFT — NOT APPROVED. Story recommended, not selected by Akhilesh. Single narrator; 30–40s target, verify voice timing after approval. English first; Roman Hinglish working conversion below.

## English draft

HDFC Bank's incoming CEO has a proposed ₹36 crore package. But how much of that is actually salary?

About ₹3.3 crore is basic salary. The fixed package also includes allowances and benefits.

Here's the bigger catch: roughly three-quarters of the total is target variable pay, linked to performance. Much of it comes as stock options, which vest over time.

So ₹36 crore isn't a guaranteed cash payment landing in his account each year. Shareholders still have to approve the package.

It's the same question worth asking about your own job offer: how much is fixed, how much is conditional, and when do you actually get it?

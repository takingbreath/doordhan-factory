## What the story infers, and what it does not

The central inference is that registration of an intermediary does not convert a GMP quote or its underlying off-exchange transaction into an exchange-backed trade. The advisory itself contrasts GMP-linked transactions with IPO participation through authorised channels. Reading a quote and making a normal IPO application are therefore kept distinct; do not tell viewers that merely checking GMP removes protection from the IPO application.

Do not claim an ordinary IPO is guaranteed profitable, every claim against an intermediary is excluded, or no civil/criminal remedy could exist. The release identifies specific regulatory protection and grievance routes for the grey-market transaction. No individual dispute, broker's conduct or contract has been investigated here.

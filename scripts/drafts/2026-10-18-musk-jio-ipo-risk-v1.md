## English script

Jio says Starlink is too expensive to compete on price. So why does satellite internet appear in Jio's IPO risk warnings?

The real comparison isn't with your mobile recharge. It's with the internet connection in your home.

Jio's argument is that its fibre and wireless broadband are cheaper today.

But its own document warns: if satellite internet gets cheaper or scales faster, those advantages may shrink. Jio could need to spend more to stay competitive.

That's the money story behind Musk versus Ambani.

An IPO isn't just a share of what a company earns today. You're also paying for what it can keep earning when a new rival arrives.

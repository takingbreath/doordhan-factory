# Yes Bank — one investor's win, another's worry

Status: DRAFT — NOT APPROVED. Recommended story for 16 October 2026; single narrator, English review first. Final target 30–40 seconds; check recorded Roman Hinglish timing. No production authorization.

## English development draft

Yes Bank's bondholders won in court. Its shareholders watched the stock fall about 14.5% on October 15. Why the opposite reactions?

Back in 2020, during the bank's rescue, ₹8,415 crore of AT1 bonds were written down to zero.

Now the Supreme Court has upheld the decision striking down that write-off. The administrator didn't have the authority to do it that way.

But a win for bondholders raises a financial question for the bank: what liabilities must it now account for?

That uncertainty worries shareholders. The final financial impact still needs clarity.

Same bank, different investors. Good news for one can be costly for another.

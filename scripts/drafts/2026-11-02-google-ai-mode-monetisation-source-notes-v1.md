## Scope corrections from the desk framing

- An analyst asked whether fewer ads explained the query/revenue disconnect; management's response established lower AI Mode monetisation, not a quantified ad-count mechanism. Do not turn the analyst's question into a company finding. The story avoids claiming exactly why each AI Mode query monetises less.
- Slower growth is not declining revenue: the retained Search line grew about 12% year on year. The sequential comparison concerns growth rates, not a claimed 12% quarter-on-quarter change.
- FX was management's largest reason for the change in Search growth from Q2 to Q3; retail/media advertising dynamics also contributed. AI Mode was one factor. The text keeps the currency qualification rather than making AI the whole explanation.
- The call describes most AI Mode queries as incremental. Do not assert that every one cannibalises an existing search or that Google proved a specific amount of revenue destruction. Ads expansion is management's stated action, not proof it will succeed.
- Google's Search-ad model does not establish Anthropic's subscription/API economics or future IPO performance. Omit a claim that investors punished Google for exactly the business Anthropic sells. Likewise, the previously verified Anthropic net-loss headline is largely a financing revaluation charge, not a cash-spend measure; do not import it as burn.

## English story for approval

OpenAI reportedly cut its fundraising target from $30 billion to $20 billion. Anthropic's approaching IPO helps explain why the timing matters.

Reuters reported that OpenAI wanted investor commitments before Anthropic's listing. Some of the same institutional investors were considering both companies, with large sums to allocate around the same time.

But there's another pressure: comparison. Anthropic's disclosures give investors a fuller picture of its business. Reuters reported that OpenAI wanted to raise money before those details invited closer comparisons.

That gives a prospective investor another option and more information before agreeing to terms. With AI stocks also under pressure, the fundraising pitch faces a tougher audience.

This doesn't establish that Anthropic caused the entire funding cut. It shows why an IPO can affect a rival before the shares even trade: it competes for the investor's cheque and gives that investor more to compare.

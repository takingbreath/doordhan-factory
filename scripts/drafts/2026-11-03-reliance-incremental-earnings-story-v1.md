## English story for approval

Reliance rallied 11.6% on Monday after its quarterly results. To understand the earnings behind that reaction, follow where the increase came from.

Its oil-to-chemicals business grew EBITDA by about 68%. Reliance says disrupted fuel supplies lifted refining spreads, while chemical margins also improved.

The important mechanism is the gap between crude costs and the value of the products sold. Higher crude prices alone don't guarantee a better refining business. Product shortages and Reliance's ability to keep sourcing and processing feedstock mattered here.

Now look at the earnings bridge. Group EBITDA increased by ₹12,042 crore over the same quarter last year. Oil-to-chemicals added ₹10,195 crore—roughly 85% of that increase.

That's 85% of the extra EBITDA, not total earnings. In a quarter with Jio's listing in the headlines, the old industrial business supplied most of the earnings increase.

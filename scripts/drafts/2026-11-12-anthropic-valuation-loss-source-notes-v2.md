## Avoid invalid shortcuts

- Do not subtract the financing expense from net loss and call the result cash burn or operating loss. The statements contain other income/expense, tax and separate mark-to-market items.
- The remaining GAAP operating loss is still an accrual accounting measure. Neither figure supplies a complete operating cash-flow or investment-return analysis.
- No claim that the full reported loss is imaginary, that dilution is harmless, that excluding one charge proves profitability, or that all such charges disappear after the IPO.
- Higher valuation is a driver in the filing, not the sole valuation-model input. Do not turn this into a guaranteed formula for the next quarter or a prediction that a lower IPO price must produce a specified gain.
- The original 29 October draft described six months. This version deliberately uses nine months. Preserve the old version as historical rather than merging its figures with these.

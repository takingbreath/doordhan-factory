## English story for approval

ESDS signed a five-year AI computing deal worth over a billion dollars.

But in this contract, ESDS is the customer.

Sharon AI supplies the Nvidia-powered computing capacity. ESDS pays for it, with the plan to sell services to its own clients.

Management says it already has onward customer commitments.

So the big number is ESDS's commitment to buy capacity—not a sales order it has won.

Its profit depends on the resale spread after costs. Its cash flow depends on when customers pay.

Follow the cash through both contracts before judging the headline.

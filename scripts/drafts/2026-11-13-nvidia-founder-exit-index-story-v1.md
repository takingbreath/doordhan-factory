## English story for approval

Jensen Huang has left Nvidia, and its CFO is taking over as interim CEO.

For Indian Nasdaq-100 investors, this reaches directly into the portfolio. Nvidia was about 8% of Motilal Oswal's Nasdaq-100 ETF at October-end.

Now the market has to judge how the business runs without its founder. Your tracker won't make that judgement for you.

Its mandate is to follow the index. A CEO departure alone isn't an automatic Nasdaq-100 removal trigger.

So the leadership changes, but your exposure can remain.

That's the trade-off: passive investing delegates stock selection. You still own the business risks of the companies the rules select.

## English script

Your next Jio recharge could matter more to its IPO story than Elon Musk.

If every existing customer generated just ten rupees more a month, that would mean roughly six thousand four hundred crore in extra annual revenue.

But Jio can't simply raise prices and assume everyone will pay.

Customers can downgrade or leave. Analysts flagged downgrades as a reason its last hike delivered weaker gains.

Jio's challenge is earning more from you without giving you a reason to leave.

What investors call growth could arrive on your phone as a more expensive recharge.

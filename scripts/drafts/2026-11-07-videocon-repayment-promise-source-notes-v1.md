## Material correction to preliminary framing

The desk suggests comparing ₹600 crore with SBI's ₹12,668 crore to describe roughly 5% repayment. That division is arithmetically possible but does not establish a recovery rate or allocation to this bank. The primary order calls the offer a combined repayment plan and records the petition's approximately 0.42% figure against admitted dues without reconciling that denominator. Omit percentages altogether; do not silently replace either figure or infer a completed haircut. The spoken story can use the reported amounts separately with their correct labels.

## Boundaries

- This is personal bankruptcy following guarantees, not proof of fraud, a criminal conviction, imprisonment or automatic cancellation of all debts.
- Do not say he has no assets or that literally everything he owns has been seized. Use the bankruptcy estate and trustee process; avoid unverified family-property claims.
- No claim that ₹600 crore was actually paid, that the full bank claim was original principal, or that the personal claim equals all Videocon-group debt.
- Rejected plan in July; subsequent bankruptcy order on 5 November. The RP's concerns explain the earlier plan failure, not a new determination that the Siliguri project was a sham.
- Do not claim a verified final creditor recovery, taxpayer bill, direct depositor loss, RBI bailout or guaranteed better recovery under bankruptcy.
- The old corporate Twin Star plan and its roughly 96% proposed haircut are excluded: they are different proceedings and must not be presented as a completed recovery plus another loss in this case.
- The ICICI–Kochhar criminal proceedings and broad claims about why Videocon failed are separate, unnecessary and not included.
- No buy/sell recommendation or personal legal advice. The closing is an editorial business insight, not a legal test that all repayment plans must satisfy in exactly this form.

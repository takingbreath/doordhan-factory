# IT shares versus the immigration headline

Status: DRAFT — NOT APPROVED. Recommended story 18 October 2026. One narrator; English review first, working Roman Hinglish below. Target final voice 30–40s; measure after approval, trim before production if needed.

## English draft

The US froze green-card filings at firms including TCS and Infosys. Then Indian IT stocks had their best week since 2008.

Nifty IT rose about 11.7% in the week ending October 16. How can bad news and a rally happen together?

First, the freeze concerns PERM, a step towards employer-sponsored green cards. It isn't a blanket cancellation of H-1B visas.

Meanwhile, quarterly results and a rotation of investor money helped lift IT shares.

The immigration problem didn't disappear. Investors were weighing other things too.

An employee's prospects of moving abroad and a company's earnings aren't the same question. One alarming headline can't answer both.

## Corrections and exclusions

- Seven consecutive years of unpaid/unclaimed dividends is not seven years without a share trade. Do not tell viewers that buy-and-hold by itself transfers their shares.
- Dividend cash and shares are different assets. The quoted value is equity market value, not a pile of rupees frozen at the transfer date. No assumed investment return or current valuation is calculated.
- An old physical certificate can be a record to investigate, not evidence by itself of current valid ownership, unsold shares, current value or a successful inheritance claim.
- The current viral certificate clips and their claimed ₹18 lakh value have not been authenticated. No family, date, return or windfall amount is used. No inference that the certificate shown in a viral clip belongs to the IEPF holdings in the study.
- Reliance's reported share of the old study and the derived ₹13,900 crore figure are unnecessary and omitted. No stock purchase/sale instruction is made.
- Transfer is not automatic forfeiture of all claim rights. Reclaiming requires the prescribed process and verification; do not promise an instant payout or a fixed processing time.

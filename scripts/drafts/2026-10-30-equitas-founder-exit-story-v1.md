## English story for approval

Equitas announced its highest-ever quarterly profit: ₹218 crore. Yet its shares fell as much as 20% that day.

The surprise was in the leadership announcement. Founder-CEO P N Vasudevan resigned, citing persistent investor concerns about a perceived conflict: his daughter co-promotes an NBFC.

His explanation matters. He says the relationship was disclosed, and there were no loans to that company—only one short-term deposit, repaid at maturity.

But investors had kept raising the issue for three years, he says. His proposed solution was to step aside and remove that overhang.

That opens another question: who replaces the man who has led Equitas since 2007? He will stay until his successor takes over, so this isn't an overnight leadership vacancy.

The tension is that removing one concern can create another. Record profits tell investors what the bank earned. They don't settle who will run it next.

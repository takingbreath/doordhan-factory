## English story for approval

On Friday, 20 November, the Sensex jumped almost 900 points, with banks leading the rally.

But across the BSE, 2,456 stocks fell. Only 1,582 rose.

The benchmark was recovering while more stocks were falling than rising.

Sensex weights its 30 companies by free-float market cap. Buying in heavyweights can lift it even when weakness is widespread elsewhere.

So “money coming into India” still leaves a question: which stocks is it reaching?

The index can recover before your portfolio does. What matters is whether the rally reaches what you own.

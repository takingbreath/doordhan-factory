## English story for approval

Google says Search queries grew at their fastest year-on-year pace in five years. Yet Search advertising revenue growth slowed.

One reason came from its own AI Mode. Google says rapid growth in those queries weighed on revenue growth because AI Mode was monetising less strongly.

That doesn't mean Search revenue fell. It still rose 12%. And AI wasn't the whole explanation: management identified currency as the biggest reason growth slowed against the previous quarter.

Google's response is to expand ads within AI Mode to more markets and follow-up questions.

That's the business tension: getting people to use a product more and earning more from that usage are separate jobs. Those additional ads are Google's attempt to close the gap.

## Movement and limits

Fast query growth → slower revenue growth → AI Mode's monetisation contribution → preserve positive revenue growth and the larger currency factor → commercial response → product adoption and monetisation can move at different speeds.

Do not state that AI Mode alone caused the slowdown, displaced a measured amount of traditional-search revenue, or explains an entire market-value fall. Management says most AI Mode queries are incremental; this is not proof that each new AI query replaces a previously lucrative search. Lower monetisation is not a disclosed per-query margin or a quantified revenue shortfall. Keep AI Mode distinct from AI Overviews, Gemini subscriptions and Google's separate advertising tools.

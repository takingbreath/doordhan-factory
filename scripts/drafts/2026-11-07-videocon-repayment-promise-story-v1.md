## English story for approval

Videocon's Venugopal Dhoot was facing SBI's ₹12,668 crore claim, including interest and penalties, after personally guaranteeing company borrowings.

His combined repayment plan offered creditors ₹600 crore over three years.

But nothing was offered upfront. The money depended on a proposed real-estate project in Siliguri: future development, approvals and cash flows that the resolution professional couldn't verify.

There was a process problem too. The plan hadn't been prepared in consultation with that professional, as required.

Creditors rejected it. On 5 November, the NCLT declared Dhoot personally bankrupt and ordered his bankruptcy estate to vest in a trustee for administration and distribution among creditors.

A repayment promise needs a credible source of cash. A big number alone doesn't supply one.

## Primary filing: contract, amount and risk

[NVIDIA Q2 FY2027 Form 10-Q, quarter ended 26 July 2026](https://investor.nvidia.com/files/doc_financials/2027/NVDA-2027-Q2-10Q-Final-including-exhibits.pdf).

Read the issuer-hosted PDF's Note 10 Additional Commitments, MD&A and relevant risk paragraphs. Locations: PDF pages 18–19 (printed 18–19), PDF page 26 (printed 26), and PDF page 34 (printed 34). A web screenshot was requested for the commitment table but the tool returned a text reference without a viewable image; verification relied on the parsed table and matching prose, not a claimed visual inspection.

Claim trace:

- Note 10's AI cloud agreements row totals **$36 billion as of 26 July 2026**. MD&A gives the same total and says duration is typically six years. These are future cloud-service commitments, not a reported loss or current-year cash outflow.
- Selected AI cloud partners purchase Nvidia infrastructure; Nvidia commits to cloud services. Partners may divert that capacity to third parties at better rates. Commitments reduce with third-party usage or Nvidia's own R&D use; revenue sharing is subject to criteria.
- The risk section expressly describes Nvidia agreeing to purchase committed capacity not sold to third parties and warns it may not be able to use/resell all of it. Thus the proposed downside is grounded in a disclosed risk, not evidence of a realised $36 billion loss.

Do not add the $36 billion line to other guarantees and call the sum debt or cash burn. Separate contracts in this filing include third-party data-centre leases, lease-default guarantees and the SB Energy arrangement. They are outside this story.

## Primary management explanation: why do it?

[NVIDIA Q2 FY2027 earnings-call transcript, 26 August 2026](https://investor.nvidia.com/files/content_files/TRANSCRIPT_-NVIDIA-Corp-NVDA-US-Q2-2027-Earnings-Call-26-August-2026-5_00-PM-ET.pdf), PDF page 5.

Management describes a minimum revenue floor over part of a facility's capacity that helps lenders finance the project, alongside participation in rental revenue above the floor. Nvidia says independent capital providers still assess deals themselves and distinguishes this model from making loans.

Supported interpretation: accepting some utilisation risk can enable additional customers' infrastructure expansion and benefit hardware sales, with potential recurring rental participation. The existence of support does not establish invented demand, improper revenue recognition, a wash trade or a guaranteed profitable outcome. Do not invent an exact multiplier from commitments to GPU sales.

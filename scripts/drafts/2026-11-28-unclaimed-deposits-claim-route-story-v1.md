## English story for approval

An old savings account sits untouched for ten years. Its balance moves from the bank to RBI's Depositor Education and Awareness Fund.

Then your family traces the account. Does that mean they have to ask RBI for the money?

RBI's UDGAM portal helps find unclaimed deposits across participating banks. But finding the account isn't the same as getting paid.

The claim still goes to the bank. It checks the claimant's entitlement, pays them, then seeks reimbursement from RBI.

So ten years is the transfer trigger, not a deadline that wipes out the claim.

Your family needs to know where to claim—not just how much you left.

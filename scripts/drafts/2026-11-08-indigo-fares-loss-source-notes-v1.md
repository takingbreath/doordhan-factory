## Calculations and labels

- Period: consolidated quarter ended 30 September 2026, compared with the year-earlier quarter. Do not label these figures November domestic-only results.
- Company PAT is a loss of ₹18,791 million = ₹1,879.1 crore, rounded to ₹1,879 crore in the story. This is reported net loss, not cash burn.
- The yield change is 34.3%, rounded to 34%. Occupancy is load factor, down 2.2 percentage points. The quarter's capacity includes non-scheduled operations.
- RASK/CASK are the company's non-Ind AS measures. The approximately ₹0.46 difference is a rounded, pre-tax unit comparison; do not multiply rounded values and call the product exact net loss or cash lost.
- Using one available seat flown one kilometre is a plain-language definition of the denominator, not a literal invoice per seat. Ancillary/other income and financing/currency effects remain in the respective reported accounting definitions.

## Essential limits

- Fuel cost growth and yield growth use different denominators. Do not place their percentage changes side by side as an exact margin calculation or call fuel CASK growth the increase in the price of jet fuel.
- No claim that the loss grew year on year. No claim that forex alone caused it, or that every forex entry is an immediate cash expense.
- Occupancy changes do not identify the entire cause of the revenue/cost gap. Demand, capacity, route mix, fuel, currency and other costs can all matter; no contribution breakdown or counterfactual profit is estimated.
- A loss does not prove fares are fair, eliminate market power, justify every surcharge or disprove an individual passenger's complaint. No collusion, deliberate scarcity or abuse finding is asserted.
- The Supreme Court remarks, alleged 700% fare rise, forthcoming hearing, duopoly share and schedule comparison are outside this one story. They are not newly verified or used as supporting conclusions. The mixed-month flight-schedule comparison must not become a clean year-on-year estimate.
- Anthropic's reported investors' preferred valuations are not a company-announced IPO range. No “valuation halved” assertion is imported from the desk. Samvat figures remain unselected and provisional at this morning's checkpoint.

## English story for approval

October's silver ETF headlines show a 57% jump in net inflows.

That sounds like more money arriving.

But funds mobilised actually fell: from ₹7,309 crore in September to ₹6,407 crore in October.

What changed even more was money going out. Redemptions dropped from ₹5,334 crore to ₹3,315 crore.

Net inflow is the difference. That difference grew because redemptions fell by more than subscriptions did.

So the 57% headline is true. It doesn't mean 57% more buying.

Before treating inflows as a vote of confidence, check what came in—and what went out.

## Factual boundaries and handoff

These are rupee flows at scheme level. They do not measure all secondary-market ETF buying, physical silver demand, numbers of people, units traded, or investor motives. Lower rupee redemptions do not prove fewer people sold, less panic, or greater conviction. Do not equate the October subscriptions with January top-buyers, apply the full January-to-November drawdown to them, or call the 57% a return.

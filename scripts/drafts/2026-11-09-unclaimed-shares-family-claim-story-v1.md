## English story for approval

That old share certificate may point to an investment your family has stopped tracking.

If dividends on those shares remain unpaid or unclaimed for seven consecutive years or more, the company must transfer the underlying shares to the Investor Education and Protection Fund, or IEPF.

This isn't just a handful of forgotten folios. A 1 Finance study put the value of shares held there at about ₹89,000 crore as of November 2025.

But transfer doesn't automatically mean the family's claim is gone. A rightful owner or eligible legal heir can apply to reclaim the shares through the prescribed process, subject to documents and verification.

Finding a certificate is a starting point, not proof of its present value or an approved claim.

The paper may be old. The right to claim may still be alive.

## English story for approval

An IPO's GMP can appear on a SEBI-registered intermediary's platform.

But that registration doesn't make the number official.

On 23 November, SEBI advised registered intermediaries and market participants to stop publishing GMP figures.

The grey-market transactions behind those quotes take place outside the exchange, without its settlement guarantee.

If such a deal goes wrong, SEBI says exchange grievance mechanisms and the ODR route aren't available.

That warning concerns grey-market deals. A normal IPO application through the authorised route is a separate transaction.

The platform's registration can't turn an off-exchange promise into an exchange-backed trade.

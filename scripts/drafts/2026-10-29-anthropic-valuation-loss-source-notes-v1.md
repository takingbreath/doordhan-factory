## Guardrails for the final manuscript

- “Non-cash” describes this remeasurement charge. It does not establish that Anthropic has no cash needs, that all losses are fictitious, or that financing obligations disappear. MD&A says remeasurement continues until conversion, repayment or other settlement.
- Do not subtract the fair-value expense from net loss and call the remainder cash burn. The cash-flow statement reports positive H1 operating cash flow, $3,929m (p169/F-13). Cash timing, investments and financing are separate questions.
- Q2 operating income is not H1 operating income. Omit the quarterly profit rather than placing it beside a six-month net loss without explanation.
- “A bigger loss doesn't always mean a worse business” is the proposed editorial inference from this mechanism, not a forecast of profitability or investment returns. The S-1 also describes substantial costs and capital requirements (pp64–65).
- Do not invent simplified contract terms, claim every instrument has identical conversion rights, or equate enterprise valuation with an IPO share price. The story only needs the disclosed fair-value mechanism.

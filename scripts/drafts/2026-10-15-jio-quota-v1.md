# Jio IPO — separate queue, no guaranteed seat

Status: DRAFT — NOT APPROVED. Recommended story for 15 October 2026. Single narrator. Target 30–40 seconds; confirm actual voice duration after Roman Hinglish approval. No production authorization.

## English development draft

Jio's IPO has a separate queue for Reliance shareholders—with shares worth up to ₹2,500 crore reserved.

But the surprising part is when eligibility was decided.

The IPO opens on October 21. This category covers eligible individual and HUF shareholders who held Reliance shares as of October 14.

So the qualifying date came before the IPO even opened.

And there's another distinction: reservation doesn't mean free shares or a guaranteed allotment. It's a separate category to apply in, subject to the offer's rules.

When an IPO headline says “reserved for you”, read it as a separate queue—not a guaranteed seat.

## Roman Hinglish working draft

Jio ke IPO mein Reliance shareholders ke liye alag queue hai. Unke liye dhai hazaar crore tak ke shares reserved hain.

Lekin twist ye hai ki eligibility ki date pehle aa chuki hai.

IPO 21 October ko khulega. Is category mein eligible individual aur HUF shareholders aate hain, jinke paas 14 October ko Reliance ke shares the.

Yaani IPO khulne se pehle qualifying date aa gayi.

Aur reservation ka matlab free shares ya pakka allotment nahi hai. Offer ke rules ke mutabik apply karne ki alag category hai.

Headline mein “reserved” dikhe, toh alag queue samajhiye—pakki seat nahi.

## Current primary mechanism

[RBI, Commercial Banks — Governance Directions, 2025, updated 1 October 2026](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13161).

Read definitions 6(2) and 6(8), and Chapter III compensation provision 62(3)(ii)(g). Clawback is a conditional contractual return of previously paid or vested remuneration; malus prevents vesting of deferred remuneration. The private-bank provisions require mechanisms for variable pay, policy triggers and an applicable period. They support the incentive explanation, not a blanket claim to all historical fixed salary or an unconditional guarantee of recovery. The final story must preserve eligibility and uncertainty.

The historical [4 November 2019 compensation circular](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/FS_Notification.aspx?Id=11720&Mode=0&fn=2) was also read. Use the current consolidated directions above for the present explanation, not the old circular as if no consolidation has occurred. No interpretation of an undisclosed individual contract or guarantee about enforceability is offered.

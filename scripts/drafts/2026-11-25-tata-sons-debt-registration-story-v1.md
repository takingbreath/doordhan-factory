## English story for approval

Tata Sons, the group's holding company, reported zero borrowings at September-end.

But as an upper-layer NBFC, it faces RBI's requirement to list within three years of classification.

It had applied to surrender its RBI registration, which could have taken it out of that framework.

Repaying the loans didn't automatically end the registration.

Reports say RBI rejected the surrender request in September, then reiterated the listing requirement in November.

So clearing the debt wasn't enough. Tata Sons also needed RBI to let it leave that regulatory category.

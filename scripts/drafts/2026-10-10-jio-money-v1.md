# Jio IPO — who receives the money?

Status: DRAFT — NOT APPROVED. Story recommendation for 10 October 2026; single narrator. No production authorization.
Target: 30–40 seconds; actual timing to be checked after spoken Roman Hinglish approval.

## English development draft

You pay Jio for your recharge. Now its IPO could raise around ₹30,000 crore. Who gets that money?

This time, existing shareholders aren't selling their shares. Jio is issuing new ones, so the money goes into the company.

But here's where a big chunk is headed: repaying loans.

The draft earmarks up to ₹27,500 crore to repay debt at Reliance Jio Infocomm.

So the story isn't just that Jio is coming to the stock market. It's what the company will do with your money.

An IPO's size tells you how much it could raise. Its purpose tells you where that money goes.

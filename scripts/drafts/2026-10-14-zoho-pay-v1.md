## English development draft

AI doesn't have to take your job to affect your salary. Zoho has paused its usual annual pay rises.

Founder Sridhar Vembu says server prices have jumped fourfold, AI usage costs are climbing, and the software outlook is uncertain.

But here's what makes this more than an AI headline: he has apologised to employees and says the company is working to avoid mass layoffs.

That isn't a promise that every job is safe. It's a pay freeze while the company tries to control costs.

When a company invests in AI, the question isn't just what the technology can do. It's who pays for the transition.

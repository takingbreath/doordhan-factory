## What is established, reported and inferred

- Established by the company announcement: the older investment-and-infrastructure relationship. “Commitment” is not proof of full disbursement, current outstanding balance or a completed purchase at today's date.
- Newly reported: the planned private placement alongside the IPO. No independent primary confirmation of its amount, participants' allocations, completion or conditions was obtained. Recheck before final words or voice; revise or remove the new amount if primary/reporting support changes.
- Inference: supplier investment can create exposure to a customer's equity value and future business at the same time. The conditional downside concerns expectations and future orders, not automatic breach/cancellation of existing commitments or a quantified loss.
- Do not claim all participants supply Anthropic directly. The reported group includes chip, memory, foundry and cloud businesses with different relationships to the ecosystem; the spoken story says “some” backers also supply it.
- No tracing of particular investment dollars back into supplier receipts has been performed. Do not label this an exact money loop, revenue fabrication, collusion or a proven bubble. Growing end-customer demand and actual service delivery still matter, but are not independently measured by this financing proposal.

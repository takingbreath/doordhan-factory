# Musk, Ambani and the distribution twist

Status: DRAFT — NOT APPROVED. Recommended story for 11 October 2026, single narrator. Target 30–40 seconds; confirm recorded duration before production.

## English development draft

Elon Musk called Mukesh Ambani “Prime Minister Ambani” over Starlink's delayed India launch. But there's a twist: Jio had signed a deal to sell Starlink.

In March last year, Jio agreed to offer Starlink through its stores and website, subject to approvals.

Now Musk is accusing Ambani of keeping it out. The government rejects selective treatment and says spectrum pricing and security requirements still have to be settled.

So this isn't a simple story of one billionaire fighting another. The company Musk is attacking was also meant to help distribute his service.

In business, the rival and the partner can be the same company.

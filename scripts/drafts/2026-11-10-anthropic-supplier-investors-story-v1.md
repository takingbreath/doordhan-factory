## English story for approval

Google, Nvidia, Microsoft and other AI infrastructure companies are reportedly planning a $26 billion private investment alongside Anthropic's IPO.

That looks like a vote of confidence. But some of those backers also sell the infrastructure Anthropic needs.

The relationship already exists. In November 2025, Microsoft and Nvidia announced investment commitments in Anthropic while Anthropic committed to buying $30 billion of Azure computing capacity powered by Nvidia.

The commercial attraction is clear: an equity stake can gain value while a growing customer buys more infrastructure.

But that connects the downside too. If Anthropic's growth disappoints, expectations for both equity returns and future infrastructure orders can weaken together.

These investors aren't exposed only to what Anthropic earns. They're also exposed to what it spends.

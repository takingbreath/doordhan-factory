## Interpretation limits

- These are different universes: Sensex's 30-company portfolio versus the broader BSE trading count. Never label all 2,456 decliners as Sensex companies, all small caps, or all Indian listed companies.
- More declining stocks does not imply a negative return for an equal-weight index without calculating returns; nor does it establish that most market capitalisation, investor wealth or individual portfolios fell. No such claim is made.
- The statement that an index can recover before a particular portfolio is a conditional implication of different holdings/weights, not an observed return for a known person's portfolio.
- The reports' global cash-rotation explanations are interpretations. These data do not trace identical investors or sale proceeds between countries. Exact FPI totals and reasons are not needed here.
- No causal claim that foreign investors caused every bank gain, no claim that the index is misleading by design, and no prediction of Monday's prices or broader participation.

## Primary rule and illustrative arithmetic

Downloaded the [company's June 2026 draft prospectus from its bookrunner](https://www.morganstanley.com/content/dam/msdotcom/en/indiaofferdocuments/pdfs/Jio_Platforms_Limited_Draft_Red_Herring_Prospectus.pdf) to temporary storage and extracted it with pdftotext. Our example is hypothetical: 150 valid applications × 2 lots = 300 lots requested; 300 ÷ 100 available = 3 times share subscription. With 100 winners among 150 eligible applicants, the probability is 100/150 = 2/3. Checked with Python Fraction arithmetic. These are invented teaching quantities, openly labelled as such; they are not Jio data or a forecast.

## Corrections and scope

The desk's 1.39 crore total applications and 70.9 lakh retail lots cover different populations. Neither their quotient nor its complement establishes retail odds. A share-subscription ratio also cannot by itself prove minimum-lot applications are oversubscribed. Quantified actual odds require the registrar's final eligible retail count and allocation.

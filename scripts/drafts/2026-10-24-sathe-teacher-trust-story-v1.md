## English story for approval

People paid Avadhut Sathe's academy to learn trading. SEBI has now ordered Sathe and the academy to refund ₹546.17 crore in fees tied to unregistered services.

The academy says it provides education. Here is the interesting part: SEBI accepts that genuine teaching happened.

But its order also finds that specific stock advice and research were delivered inside the courses and connected WhatsApp interactions, without the required registration.

That combination matters. A recommendation can feel more credible when it comes from the person who taught you how to judge it. SEBI explains that the student's trust in a mentor cannot replace safeguards around suitability, research and conflicts of interest.

That is why the refund concerns fees for the services identified in the order; it does not reimburse every participant's trading losses.

The takeaway: learning from someone can increase your confidence in their next trade recommendation. That confidence makes the protections around the advice more important.

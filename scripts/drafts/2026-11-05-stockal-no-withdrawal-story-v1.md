## English story for approval

At Stockal, some investors say the US shares they chose were sold without their consent. One investor's Apple, Tesla and Nvidia holdings were among those liquidated. The proceeds went into 300,000 shares of Chinese microcap Zhibao. Its price then fell nearly 60%, according to Moneycontrol's report.

Here's the part that makes this different: Borderless, Stockal's parent, says no funds were withdrawn. It acknowledges unauthorized trades in 13 accounts, with large-cap holdings replaced by thinly traded microcaps.

So the reported damage didn't need money to leave the account. It happened through a change in what the account owned.

Borderless says it found no breach of its own systems; it says valid credentials appear to have been used. The cause and responsibility remain under investigation.

But valid credentials aren't the same as an authorized trade. The risk is losing control of what you own, even when there is no withdrawal.

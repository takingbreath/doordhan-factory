## English story for approval

Jio is scheduled to list tomorrow, with a reported equity valuation of around ₹10.3 lakh crore at the issue price. That makes its potential place in a large-cap index worth asking about.

But Nifty 50 is not simply a list of the fifty biggest headline valuations. Its selection rules consider eligible companies, liquidity and free-float market value.

That last distinction matters. Shares held by promoters or strategic owners do not contribute to the investible calculation in the same way as shares available to public investors. The whole-company value cannot tell us Jio's future index weight.

And inclusion is a separate decision under the index rules. A Nifty 50 tracker follows the selected basket; an IPO listing does not automatically add the company's own shares to it.

For the index investor, the relevant next event is an announced index change and its effective date. The listing headline alone does not establish either.

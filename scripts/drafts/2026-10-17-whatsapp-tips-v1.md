# The free tip — who benefits?

Status: DRAFT — NOT APPROVED. Recommended alternate to desk's IDBI pick for 17 October 2026. One narrator. 30–40s target, verify final Roman Hinglish voice timing before production.

## English development draft

Every day, WhatsApp groups sent “multibagger” stock tips. Buyers of two shares crossed one lakh. Then the groups disappeared.

Within days, both stocks lost about 70%.

The ED is investigating an alleged manipulation scheme. Its reported findings point to something that happened before those tips: selected investors had already received shares, and prices were pushed up while holdings stayed concentrated.

Then came the daily recommendations to ordinary buyers.

On October 16, the ED searched 29 premises. The investigation is still ongoing.

A free stock tip can cost you money. Before asking how much you could make, ask who benefits when you buy.

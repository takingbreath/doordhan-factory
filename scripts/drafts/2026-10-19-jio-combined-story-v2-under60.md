An extra ₹48 in monthly ARPU would mean roughly ₹31,000 crore a year for Jio—about the size of its IPO.

The challenge is collecting more, without customers downgrading or leaving.

Jio's other growth lever is home broadband. That's where Starlink overlaps.

Today, satellite pricing and shared capacity limit the threat. But if those economics improve, Starlink could compete for homes AirFiber is targeting.

It doesn't need to replace Jio's mobile network to affect that opportunity.

For investors, the question is how profitably Jio can monetise existing customers—and win the next ones.

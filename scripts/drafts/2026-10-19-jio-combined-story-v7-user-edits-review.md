The new price lead also opens a second question (what Starlink costs) while the payoff is about Jio's future earnings. Recommendation: begin with the user's existing Musk/IPO sentence and omit the separate price lead for this version. [Freshly opened Business Standard report on Musk's affordability statement](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/starlink-services-in-india-to-be-affordable-spacex-founder-elon-musk-126101100722_1.html). - x% can be filled with approximately 5.6%, specifically FY26 mobile-broadband CUSTOMER growth, not "mobile services" revenue growth. Primary [October SEBI abridged prospectus](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/commondocs/oct-2026/Jio%20Platforms%20Limited%20-%20Red%20Herring%20Prospectus%20and%20Abridged%20Prospectus_p.pdf), PDF page 13, note 1: March 2026 mobile broadband customers 496.3m, March 2025 469.8m; calculation (496.3/469.8 − 1) × 100 = 5.6407%. Fixed-broadband customers increased 55.19% over the same FY26 period, per the issuer prospectus verified in v1. This compares growth rates on different-sized customer bases; it does not establish revenue growth or revenue contribution.

- Keep the SIM statement explicitly current/India-specific; do not imply Starlink has no Direct-to-Cell business anywhere or cannot enter mobile in future. The user's preceding "filhaal" helps, but a production wording should retain that boundary. The 53 crore figure is total Jio connections, not exclusively mobile users.

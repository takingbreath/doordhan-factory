# Where did ₹10 lakh crore go?

## English development draft

On October 8, roughly ₹10 lakh crore disappeared from the stock market. Who got that money?

There isn't a matching ₹10 lakh crore payout to someone else. The headline measures a fall in market value.

Here's the twist: every share doesn't have to be sold for that value to fall.

A company's market value uses its share price multiplied by all its shares. When trades happen at a lower price, that lower price changes the valuation of the whole company.

So a market crash can shrink your portfolio without anyone touching your shares. The headline counts the value that fell—not cash someone collected.

Lekin twist ye hai: iske liye har share bikna zaroori nahi.

Company ki market value nikalti hai share price ko uske saare shares se multiply karke. Jab kam price par trading hoti hai, usi price par poori company ki value dobara gini jaati hai.

Isliye aapke shares bina bike bhi portfolio ki value gir sakti hai. Headline giri hui value ginti hai, kisi ko mile hue cash ko nahi.

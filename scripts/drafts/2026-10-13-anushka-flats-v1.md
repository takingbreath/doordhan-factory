# Anushka–Kohli family flats: price versus possession

Status: DRAFT — NOT APPROVED. Recommended story for 13 October 2026. Single narrator, creator-format proposal. Target final voice: 30–40 seconds, verify recorded timing before production.

## English development draft

Anushka Sharma and her family, including Virat Kohli, have bought three Mumbai flats worth about ₹44 crore. But they can't move in yet.

Possession is expected by 2031.

The flats are on the same floor. The family has already paid about ₹2.5 crore in stamp duty, while payments for the homes are linked to construction progress.

Here's the part the price tag misses: signing for a home and getting its keys can be years apart.

We don't know their reason for buying. But the decision raises a question for any buyer: you're choosing a price—and how long you can wait.

## 1. Supplier-side primary disclosure

[SharonAI SEC registration amendment, agreement description and customer disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2068385/000149315226039475/forms-1a.htm).

Opened the filing and relevant business/contract passages. It identifies ESDS and certain subsidiaries as the **customer** of Sharon's managed GPU-compute/cloud-infrastructure services. The agreement was entered into on 31 March 2026 and announced on 1 April. The initial term is 60 months from the service-start date, with a possible additional 24 months.

The issuer's summary calls it about US$1.25 billion; the detailed service-order description uses approximately US$1.26 billion. It specifies monthly service fees in advance and US$140 million of letters of credit/bank guarantees. These are procurement/security terms for ESDS, not an ESDS customer-sales order or profit. The proposed story avoids exact-value and security-detail detours.

The cash-direction explanation does not imply all fees are due immediately or that the full contract is a recognised loss. It concerns computing services, not a claim that ESDS owns the physical cluster.

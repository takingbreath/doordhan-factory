## Dialogue

Musk is publicly fighting Ambani just as Jio prepares to go public. If you're looking at the IPO, how worried should you be about Starlink?

Look, they mostly serve different customers today. You're not buying a Starlink dish instead of a Jio recharge.

Yet Jio flags satellites as a risk. So where could they end up competing?

Jio already has over 53 crore customers. Getting them to pay more isn't as simple as hiking recharge prices. Some just switch to a cheaper plan.

Home broadband gives Jio another way to grow. Its customer base there grew 55% last financial year.

AirFiber can reach homes where laying fibre is difficult. Starlink could make sense for some of those same homes.

But Starlink still needs to get cheaper and add capacity. If it does, Jio could have to offer better deals or spend more to win those homes.

So Musk doesn't have to take away Jio's existing mobile customers. He could make its next phase of growth less profitable.

## English story for approval

Jio's allotment is scheduled today. Retail subscription reports are around 2.7 times. Dividing one by that number seems to give your chance—but that counts shares requested, not applicants.

Suppose an IPO has 100 lots available. There are 150 valid retail applicants, each asking for two lots. Demand is 300 lots: three times the supply.

Yet 100 of those 150 applicants can receive one lot. Two-thirds succeed, even though the issue is three times subscribed.

For the actual odds, we need eligible retail applications and the lots available to them. The total across every investor category cannot replace that retail count.

The subscription multiple measures demand. Your allotment chance depends on the competing eligible applications in your category.

# E16 song guide for Instagram Edits (whole seconds)

Song: Zindagi Ek Safar Hai Suhana (Andaz, 1971), Kishore Kumar. Add the song three times, one clip per row.

| Clip | Put it on the reel at | Ends on the reel at | Start the song at | Volume |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 | 0:52 | 0:26 | FULL for 0:00-0:01, then LOW to the end of the clip (split it at 0:01) |
| 2 | 0:54 | 1:45 | 2:06 | LOW |
| 3 | 1:46 | 1:52 | 0:20 | FULL to the end of the reel |

Silence (no song): 0:52-0:54 (the brake) and 1:45-1:46 (the outro brake).
Volume: FULL = as loud as Pandey's voice (start near 60% and match by ear); LOW = well under his voice (start near 15%).
Loop: clip 3 ends at song 0:26 and clip 1 starts at song 0:26, so the song runs on when the reel loops.

## 2. Camera setup
Rashmi is filmed like the editing reference: a full-frame mirrorless cinema camera on a tripod that she moves for each
angle (self-shot, so she talks straight to the lens), not a phone selfie. The camera never appears in frame.
- **Lenses:** 24 mm f/1.4 for the high, low and overhead angles (a little wide-angle perspective, hands near the lens
  grow); 35 mm f/1.4 for the eye-level medium; 50 mm f/1.2 for the close-up (soft background).
- **Settings in the prompts:** shallow depth of field, a gentle handheld or tripod feel as each shot needs, 30 fps
  (the format's rate; the reference is 30 fps), a 180-degree shutter look (natural motion blur on fast hands).
- **Lip-sync limits stay:** each talking frame keeps her face 20-28% of the frame height and close to frontal (preflight
  checks yaw, pitch and roll). An extreme angle that fails preflight gets a gentler version of itself.

## 6. Edit and finish
- **Cuts:** rhythmic in the hook (about 0.7 s); later cuts land on her gestures (cutting to motion); light-leak flashes at
  5.2 s, 35.6 s and 49.0 s; R4's hand flick wipes into the tease; the Vox desk scenes keep the curve cuts
  (assets/edits/vox_desk/README.md).
- **Kinetic type:** a condensed bold uppercase in yellow (Anton, free licence) under a small white handwritten word
  (Caveat, free licence), entering with motion blur. On her shots it sits behind her head (a person matte from
  MediaPipe selfie segmentation); never over her face.
- **Captions:** small pale-yellow serif (Playfair Display Bold, in assets/fonts), sentence case, phrase by phrase from
  the Scribe word times, y 860-1020, at most 580 px wide.
- **Grade:** warm filmic: soft contrast, warm skin, slightly teal shadows, a little halation on highlights, a light
  vignette, fine grain (light on her face for the face check), 30 fps. No old-TV look.
- **Safe box:** everything important inside x 40-640, y 170-855; the DOORDHAN bug top right.
- **Sound:** the voice is the approved v3 (clean, no room tone). Tonal effects sit about 18-20 dB under the voice while
  she speaks and come up in the pauses. Loudness: measure, one gain and a limiter to -14 LUFS.

## 7. Making the tonal sounds
ElevenLabs sound generation for the instruments (piano, cello, pizzicato, celesta, choir, bell); measure each note's
pitch (parselmouth) and retune it to D minor locally, so every sound sits in one key and the reel plays like a small
score. Plain foley (paper, stamp, shutter, clink) from the kit in assets/audio. No song: Akhilesh may lay one on top
(a song in D minor or F major fits the effects).

## 10. Risks
- **Unusual angles and lip sync:** the overhead and low-angle shots are new for Wan. Each keyframe must pass preflight
  (face 20-28%, close to frontal) before any video credit; a failed shot falls back to an eye-level framing.
- **The budget is tight** (about 58 of 60): retakes are the risk. Any overrun needs Akhilesh first.
- **Two founders may have no good photo** (Sam McCandlish, Tom Brown): silhouettes then.
- **The tonal sounds are a first for us:** if the retuned notes sound synthetic, the fallback is plain foley.

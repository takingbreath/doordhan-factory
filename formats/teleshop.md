# Template: Abhi Call Karein! (1990s teleshopping)

**Premise:** an over-the-top 1990s Indian teleshopping host sells a financial product live on air to a caller. The joke is always on the seller and the fine print; the caller is the everyman. The finance message rides on the reveal.
**When to use it:** products and pitches with hidden costs or tricks, like "No-Cost EMI", "guaranteed returns" plans, insurance-plus-investment, credit card offers, and any marketing that makes a number look smaller than it is. (EMI and loan topics are resting for now.) Episode 1: iPhone 18 Pro on EMI.

## Look and graphics
Pink 90s studio, price graphic, lower third, starbursts and fine-print strips; the DOORDHAN coin bug.
Built locally in Episode 1: the "5 TAREEKH / SUBAH 6:02" title card, the HUM FINANCE end slate (assets/cards/hum_finance_slate.png) with scrolling fine-print jokes for anyone who pauses, and the DOORDHAN end ident (logo at assets/logos/doordhan_logo.png on studio pink).

## Sound
The presenter's lines levelled with the caller's, which was a big early fix. Landline EQ on the caller. A fast, flat, garbled-sounding T&C read over the slate is part of the joke.

## Shot pattern that worked (Episode 1)
1. The pitch with the price graphic, a crash zoom on the product, and he picks up the ringing phone.
2. Caller full frame; presenter full frame with snap zooms on his punchlines.
3. The escalation: a starburst, then fine-print strips slamming in one by one, then a crash zoom on the grin.
4. The confession: a crash zoom on the smug grin; the caller's face falls on the reveal (a reaction shot of whoever the joke lands on).
5. The payoff that proves the fine print (a card, the SMS), then the in-world brand slate with the T&C voice-over, then the ident.

## Recurring devices
HUM FINANCE, "उम्र भर फ़ाइनेंस" ("finance for life"), the in-world lender; "Terms and conditions apply"; fine-print jokes that reward pausing; Sachdev's "हमें पता है" ("we know").

Read the supplied state, verified research, chosen format, playbook and story/prosody reference. Research is factual
input; its narrative suggestions are optional. Preserve any hook or story wording the human has already selected.

Write one short opening and the English story that fulfils it, for the audience and duration request in the state.
At story-development stage clarity and connected reveals take priority over the final word budget. Establish the
viewer question, the successive changes of understanding and the final insight. Apply the playbook's curiosity,
contrast, density, audience and prosody lessons without inventing stakes or forcing a beat count. Do not write the
finished spoken script, choose extra characters or generate voice. Use the chosen format as context, not a reason
to replace the user's storyline with Pandey's helpline.

The story file contains the proposed hook and story only, labelled DRAFT — HOOK AND STORY NOT APPROVED. The notes
file contains the question/payoff, why this angle, material uncertainties, source boundaries and missing inputs.
Keep it short. Do not overwrite an existing story or manual edits: report that the run must be continued instead.
Never manufacture approval; the coordinator presents these files and waits for a human decision.

Reply with the paths written, or BLOCKED and why.
